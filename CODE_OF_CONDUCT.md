# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy and kindness, being respectful of differing opinions,
giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is
best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via GitHub.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
