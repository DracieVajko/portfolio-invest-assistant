## Summary

Monolithic milestone that expanded the baseline with multi-source research and portfolio auto-discovery, while remaining a single-script architecture.

- Extended `portfolio_ai_assistant.py` (multi-source `yfinance` / `DDGS` / `finvizfinance`, news relevance scoring, portfolio auto-discovery, deterministic signals with data-quality scoring)
- Trading 212 read-only integration (GET-only, no order placement, no modification or cancellation)
- Same safe template pattern: `api.env.example`, `portfolio_config.example.json`, gitignored local `api.env` / `portfolio_config.json` / `PIEs/*.csv` / `data/` / `logs/` / `reports/`
- `requirements.txt` and `run_*.bat` helpers

## Scope and limitations

- Historical milestone; preserved for lineage. Not the current stable release (`v0.6.0`).
- No full automated test suite in this snapshot; live providers may be unavailable or rate-limited.
- Read-only research and advisory tool — not financial advice and not an automated trading system.
